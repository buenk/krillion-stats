How to capture a dive

  1. 1

    Finish the daily dive

    Play on krillion.io until the results screen.

  2. 2

    Click the bookmarklet

    It reads your result and today's field stats. Field stats only exist on the day itself, so capture before the 05:00 UTC reset.

  3. 3

    Save to stats site

    The panel's button opens this page with the new dive added.

Capture dive

Drag onto your bookmarks bar, or save it with a keyword (e.g. ks) and type that on the results screen. Standalone version if the normal one won't load.

Backup

Your dives live in this browser's storage. Export a copy now and then; commit it as data.json to see it on other devices (the repo is public).

Not affiliated with Krillion.Data comes from your browser and the public endpoints the game's results screen uses.
